================================================================
  Command Center - Setup Guide
================================================================

This is a personal study app that runs ON YOUR OWN computer. Nothing
about your account leaves your machine. It pulls your deadlines, grades,
and files from OAKS, and lets you chat with an AI about your course
materials.

Works on Windows 10/11 (Intel/AMD). You do NOT need admin rights.


BEFORE YOU START (2 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Get a FREE AI key (Google Gemini):
   - Go to  https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
   - Sign in with a PERSONAL Gmail (NOT your school @cofc.edu account -
     school accounts are usually blocked).
   - Click "Create API key" and copy it. You'll paste it during setup.

2. (Optional) Your Google Calendar:
   - In Google Calendar > Settings > (your calendar) >
     "Secret address in iCal format", copy that link.
   - Paste it during setup and your personal events show alongside
     your course deadlines.

3. Log into OAKS (https://lms.cofc.edu) in Chrome and stay logged in -
   the app uses that to find your courses and sync deadlines.


INSTALL (about 10 minutes, mostly waiting on a download)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere (e.g. your Desktop).
2. Double-click  install.bat
   - If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click
     "More info" then "Run anyway" (it's unsigned, but it only sets up
     the app locally).
   - It installs Python, downloads the AI libraries (~1 GB, one time),
     then asks you a few questions:
        your name, your city, pick "1) Gemini", paste your Gemini key,
        your courses (it can find them from OAKS automatically), and
        your Google Calendar link if you want.

3. When it finishes, load the browser helper (syncs deadlines/grades):
     - Open Chrome, go to   chrome://extensions
     - Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right)
     - Click "Load unpacked" and pick the  browser-extension  folder
       inside:  C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\SchoolHub


USING IT
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Double-click the "Command Center" icon on your Desktop. A small window
  opens and your app appears in your browser at 127.0.0.1:8177.
- To add course files (PDFs, slides, notes), drop them into the matching
  folder under:  C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\SchoolHub\materials\
  They get read in automatically the next time you open the app.
- Closing the little window quits the app.


SOMETHING WRONG? RUN THE CHECKUP FIRST
----------------------------------------------------------------
The app can diagnose itself. Open PowerShell (Start menu, type
"PowerShell") and paste this one line:

   & "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\SchoolHub\.venv\Scripts\python.exe" -m brain doctor

It prints every check as OK / WARN / FAIL and tells you what to do
about anything that failed. Fix the FIRST failure - the rest usually
clear up with it.

It also saves a copy to:

   %LOCALAPPDATA%\SchoolHub\data\diagnostic.txt

Send that file to whoever set you up. It is safe to send: it describes
your API key and course logins ("present, 39 characters") but never
includes the actual keys, passwords, or cookies.

Add --offline on the end if you have no internet.


COMMON PROBLEMS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Chat says it cannot answer: almost always the AI key. The checkup
  tests it and says whether it is missing or was rejected. A key made
  with a SCHOOL Google account gets rejected - make a new one with a
  personal Gmail.
- Search finds nothing: no files in it yet. Drop PDFs and slides into
  the course folders, then open the app again.
- I missed the Google Calendar question during setup: add it any time.
  Copy your link (Google Calendar > Settings > your calendar > "Secret
  address in iCal format") and run, with YOUR link in the quotes:

     & "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\SchoolHub\.venv\Scripts\python.exe" -m brain calendar link "PASTE_YOUR_LINK_HERE"

- Deadlines stopped updating: your course-site login expired. Log into
  OAKS in Chrome again and the helper picks it up.
- Nothing opens: run the checkup above, then look in
  C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\SchoolHub\data\logs\server.log
  and send it to whoever gave you this.
- The browser extension turned itself off after a Chrome update: just
  redo the "Load unpacked" step above.
- ARM PC (Snapdragon / Copilot+): not supported yet - needs an
  Intel/AMD computer.

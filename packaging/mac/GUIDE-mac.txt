================================================================
  Command Center - Mac Setup Guide
================================================================

A personal study app that runs ON YOUR OWN Mac. Nothing about your
accounts leaves your machine. It pulls your deadlines, grades, and
course files from OAKS, and lets you chat with an AI about your notes.

REQUIREMENTS
  - Any Mac from roughly 2017 on:
      Apple Silicon (M1 or newer): macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer
      Intel Mac:                   macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer
  - Chrome installed (the sync helper only works in Chrome)
  - No admin password needed. Nothing is installed system-wide.
  - Built for College of Charleston. Deadline sync talks to
    lms.cofc.edu, so at another school the chat and your own files
    still work, but automatic deadlines will not.


BEFORE YOU START (2 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Get a FREE AI key:
   - Go to  https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
   - Sign in with a PERSONAL Gmail. Do NOT use your @cofc.edu
     account - school Google accounts are blocked from this, and the
     app will not be able to answer anything.
   - Click "Create API key" and copy it. You'll paste it during setup.

2. (Optional) Your Google Calendar:
   - Google Calendar > Settings > (your calendar) >
     "Secret address in iCal format" - copy that link.
   - Paste it during setup and your personal events show up next to
     your course deadlines.

3. Log into OAKS (lms.cofc.edu) in Chrome and stay logged in.
   Do this BEFORE running the installer - setup can then find your
   courses automatically instead of asking you to type them.


INSTALL (about 10 minutes, mostly waiting on a download)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip the folder you were sent into your Downloads.

2. Open Terminal (press Cmd+Space, type "Terminal", hit Return).

3. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal, and press Return:

       bash ~/Downloads/CommandCenter-Setup-Mac/install.sh

   (If you unzipped somewhere else, drag the install.sh file onto the
   Terminal window instead of typing the path - that fills it in.)

   Why Terminal instead of double-clicking? Because a double-click on a
   downloaded script makes macOS show a scary "unidentified developer"
   block. Running it this way skips that entirely. Nothing else about
   it is different.

4. It will ask you a few questions:
       your name, your city, pick "1) Gemini", paste your Gemini key,
       your courses (it can find them from OAKS automatically), and
       your Google Calendar link if you want one.

5. When it finishes, load the browser helper (one time only):
       - Open Chrome, go to   chrome://extensions
       - Turn ON "Developer mode" (top right)
       - Click "Load unpacked", and choose the folder the installer
         printed at the end. You can paste this into the file dialog
         with Cmd+Shift+G:
         ~/Library/Application Support/CommandCenter/browser-extension

   Skipping this step is allowed - the app still works - but your
   deadlines and grades will stop updating after a day or two,
   because nothing is refreshing your course-site logins.


USING IT
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Open "Command Center" from your Applications folder. Drag it to your
  Dock so it's one click from then on.
- Your course files live in a folder called "Command Center" in your
  home folder. Drop PDFs, slides, and notes into the right course
  folder - the app reads them in next time you open it.
- Closing the little window quits the app.


SOMETHING WRONG? RUN THE CHECKUP FIRST
----------------------------------------------------------------
The app can diagnose itself. Copy this ENTIRE line into Terminal and
press Return (it is one line, and the quotes matter):

   "$HOME/Library/Application Support/CommandCenter/venv/bin/python" -m brain doctor

It prints a list of checks with OK / WARN / FAIL, and tells you what
to do about anything that failed. Fix the FIRST failure listed - the
rest usually clear up on their own once it is fixed.

It also saves a copy to:

   ~/Library/Application Support/CommandCenter/data/diagnostic.txt

Send that file to whoever set you up and they can tell what's wrong
without touching your Mac. It is safe to send: it describes your API
key and course logins ("present, 39 characters") but never includes
the actual keys, passwords, or cookies.

Add --offline to the end if you have no internet and just want the
local checks.


COMMON PROBLEMS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- "It needs macOS 13/14 or newer": update macOS in System Settings >
  General > Software Update, then run the install line again.

- Nothing happens when I open the app: run the checkup above. If it
  says another program is using port 8177, quit that program, or
  open the app from Terminal on a different port:
     CC_PORT=8178 "$HOME/Library/Application Support/CommandCenter/venv/bin/python" \
       "$HOME/Library/Application Support/CommandCenter/launch.py"

- Chat says it cannot answer / nothing comes back: almost always the
  AI key. The checkup actually tests it and will say whether the key
  is missing or was rejected. A key made with a SCHOOL Google account
  gets rejected - make a new one with a personal Gmail.

- Search finds nothing: you have no files in it yet. Put PDFs and
  slides into the course folders inside ~/Command Center, then open
  the app again (it indexes on open).

- Deadlines stopped updating: your course-site login expired. Just log
  into OAKS in Chrome again - the helper picks it up automatically.
  If the checkup says the Chrome helper is missing, redo the
  "Load unpacked" step above.

- I missed the Google Calendar question during setup: you can add it
  any time without redoing setup. Copy your link (Google Calendar >
  Settings > your calendar > "Secret address in iCal format"), then run
  this one line with YOUR link in the quotes:

     "$HOME/Library/Application Support/CommandCenter/venv/bin/python" -m brain calendar link "PASTE_YOUR_LINK_HERE"

  It connects the calendar and imports your events immediately.

- My personal calendar events are missing: same command as above - the
  link either was never added or was pasted wrong.

- The first question takes forever: the search model (~130 MB)
  downloads once. After that it is instant, and works offline.

- The install stopped partway (internet dropped): just run the same
  Terminal line again. It picks up where it left off and will not
  lose your settings or your files.
